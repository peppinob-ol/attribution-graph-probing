\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{1.\ No grouping-method baseline.}
We compare labeled supernodes against matched random controls rather than against geometric or learned clustering: a behavior-grounded grouping beats a geometric one on behavior-grounded metrics by construction, and there is no canonical feature-clustering baseline in the SAE/CLT literature. The harness (\S\ref{sec:method:harness}) is method-agnostic and we encourage future groupings to be evaluated on it.

\paragraph{2.\ Attention-circuit blindspot.}
Attribution graphs freeze attention; our swaps run with attention free. The asymmetry plausibly accounts for part of the residual miss rate; attention-aware variants are future work (\cref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{3.\ Substring matching and multi-token heuristics.}
The concept-to-supernode matcher uses bidirectional substring matching, which admits spurious containment (e.g.\ \texttt{is} $\subset$ \texttt{mississippi}). We audited the behavior and corrected unambiguous mismatches before the sweep; tightening the matcher remains future work.

\paragraph{4.\ Threshold sensitivity is unmeasured.}
Decision-rule thresholds were iteratively refined on pilot circuits; we did not run a formal $\pm 10$--$20\%$ sensitivity sweep. Thresholds are configurable defaults in the released code.

\paragraph{5.\ Cross-prompt robustness is observational.}
The early-vs-late layer-overlap pattern (\S\ref{sec:results:diagnostic:layers}) is descriptive: we did not directly intervene on early- vs late-layer features to verify the backbone-and-specialization reading.

\paragraph{6.\ Within-domain pair-level scaffold prediction is null in USA.}
Scaffold compatibility orders our four domains exactly but does not predict pair-level Hit-rate within USA, plausibly because the within-domain scaffold range is too narrow. The scaffold claim is between-domain only (\cref{sec:results:negative}).

\paragraph{7.\ Domain scope: two-hop factual recall only.}
Our four evaluation domains are all two-hop factual-recall prompts. Whether the pipeline transfers to relational tasks with different structure (multi-hop reasoning, antonymy, code, dialogue, instruction-following) is untested.

\paragraph{8.\ Three levels of interpretive claim; single model and language.}
In the three-level framing of \citet{geiger2025causal} we claim Levels~1 and~2 only; Level~3 (full mechanistic identification of latent variables) is disclaimed. All experiments use Gemma-2-2B-it with CLT-HP on English (\cref{sec:method:domains}); cross-architecture, cross-dictionary, and multilingual generalization are open.

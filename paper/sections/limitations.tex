\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

The workshop call is explicit that limitations must be load-bearing. We enumerate honestly.

\paragraph{1.\ No grouping-method baseline.}
We deliberately do not compare against geometric clustering (cosine, layer-adjacency, $k$-means). Any grouping derived from a behavior-grounded signature beats a geometric grouping on behavior-grounded metrics by construction (structurally biased in our favor), and there is no canonical feature-clustering baseline in the SAE/CLT literature. We instead validate via intervention against matched random controls (\cref{def:matched}), which is method-agnostic and applicable to any future grouping. We encourage others to run our harness on competing groupings.

\paragraph{2.\ Attention-circuit blindspot.}
Attribution graphs freeze attention; swaps run free. The asymmetry plausibly explains part of the 33--79\% miss rate in the strong domains (\citealp{ameisen2025circuittracing}). Attention-aware variants are listed as future work (\cref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{3.\ Sounds dataset structural issues.}
Six entities, $12/30$ pairs share answers, $K{=}6$. Sounds fails Condition~2 of \cref{def:opuseful} (\cref{sec:results:negative}); excluded from cross-domain inference for C1.

\paragraph{4.\ Substring matching and multi-token heuristics.}
The concept-to-supernode matcher allows reverse containment (e.g., \texttt{is} matching \texttt{mississippi}), affecting 28--45\% of entity-field combinations. Multi-word answers resolve to first subword; both can miss or false-match. Impact on swap performance is unmeasured.

\paragraph{5.\ Threshold sensitivity is unmeasured.}
Decision-rule thresholds were iteratively refined on pilot circuits; no formal $\pm 10$--$20\%$ sensitivity sweep. Thresholds are treated as configurable defaults.

\paragraph{6.\ Cross-prompt robustness is observational.}
$N{=}1{,}607$ pairs but observational; we do not run interventions on the overlap claim itself; the layer-gradient evidence for C2.3 is correlational.

\paragraph{7.\ Within-domain pair-level scaffold prediction is null in USA.}
Reported as a sub-claim negative (\cref{sec:results:negative}); the scaffold story is between-domain only.

\paragraph{8.\ Three levels of interpretive claim; single model and language.}
Per~\citet{geiger2025causal}, we claim Levels 1--2 only. Level~3 (full mechanistic identification) is disclaimed. All experiments use Gemma-2-2B-it with CLT-HP, English single-hop prompts; cross-architecture, cross-dictionary, and multilingual generalization are open.
